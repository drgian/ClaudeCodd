NG CRD as-is — takes the CRD on whatever NG org the contact is linked to and compares it directly to the waterfall CRD. The problem: most contacts in NG are linked to an office node. Offices are branches or sub-locations — they are not FINRA-registered entities and have no CRD of their own. So the vast majority of contacts land in “NG org has no CRD” and cannot be compared at all. Only the smaller set of contacts already linked directly to a firm (a FINRA-registered entity) appear as conflicts here.

NG CRD walked up — for contacts linked to an office, this view follows the NG org hierarchy one level up to the parent firm, then uses that firm’s CRD for the comparison. Firms are FINRA-registered and carry a CRD, which means most of the previously incomparable contacts can now be evaluated. Contacts already linked to a firm are unchanged. All comparisons are exact CRD only — no name matching.
