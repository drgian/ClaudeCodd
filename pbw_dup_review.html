Why these duplicates exist

Two Salesforce instances, one person

SFDC_GWM (Global Wealth Management) and SFDC_NG (Next Gen) both track the same advisor. Each Salesforce has its own contact record — same CRD, email, and phone — but xDM creates two separate golden records instead of merging them.

ExactEmail rule excludes SFDC_NG

The current ExactEmail match rule has a guard clause: Record1.PublisherID <> 'SFDC_NG'. This blocks email-based matching for the primary CRM, so GWM and NG records with identical email addresses never match.

CRD not flowing from GWM to xDM

ExactCRD (score 100) would auto-merge any two masters with the same CRD. The 559 unmerged pairs suggest CRD is not being populated for SFDC_GWM master records in xDM, even though it appears in the Snowflake enriched view.

Every pair is definitively the same person

FINRA CRD numbers are unique lifetime identifiers. Two records with the same non-null CRD are the same individual — no exceptions. 152 of the 559 pairs also have identical email, phone, and ZIP on both sides.
