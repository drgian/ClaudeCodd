Why these duplicates exist

Two Salesforce instances, one person

SFDC_GWM (Global Wealth Management) and SFDC_NG (Next Gen) both track the same advisor. Each instance has its own contact record with the same CRD, email, and phone — but xDM treats them as two separate people and creates two golden records.

ExactEmail rule excludes SFDC_NG

The current ExactEmail match rule has a guard clause: Record1.PublisherID <> 'SFDC_NG'. This explicitly prevents SFDC_NG from participating in email-based matching — so even when GWM and NG records share the same email, no match fires.

CRD not flowing from GWM to xDM

ExactCRD (score 100) would auto-merge any two records with the same CRD. The fact that 559 same-CRD pairs remain unmerged indicates the CRD field is not being populated for SFDC_GWM master records in xDM, even though it appears in the Snowflake enriched view.

Every pair is definitively the same person

FINRA CRD numbers are unique lifetime identifiers. Two records with the same non-null CRD are the same individual — no exceptions. Of the 559 pairs, 152 also have identical email, phone, and ZIP on both sides, making identification completely unambiguous.
