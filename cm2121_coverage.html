Methodology

How this analysis was built

Data source

The 3,083 pairs come from the PB/Wires duplicate workbook (Snowflake, run 2026-08-05). Pairs were generated by blocking on shared CRD, shared email, shared phone, and shared xDM business team, then scored with Jaro-Winkler and classified:

BucketClassification rule
DefiniteShared email + JW(full name) ≥ 80 — OR — shared email + shared phone — OR — shared phone + JW(full name) ≥ 85 + no CRD conflict
LikelyExactLastName + JW(full name) ≥ 75 (or ≥ 90 for common last names) + at least one of: shared phone / email / ZIP / address / biz team
Name CollisionEmails differ only by middle initial or trailing digit — same name, different people at same firm

CM-2121 rule simulation notes

R10 and R11 are approximated using column-level signals from the workbook CSV: exact/fuzzy last name, shared phone count, shared team count, ZIP match, and address match. The JW(first) ≥ 0.75 guard cannot be evaluated directly without first-name strings in all rows, so Likely and Definite bucket membership serves as a proxy — all pairs in those buckets have JW ≥ 75 as part of their classification. ExactEmail and ExactFNLNSfxZip have publisher restrictions that cannot be modeled precisely at pair level — counts for those rules are upper bounds.

Recall — pairs we may have missed

The 3,083 pairs represent only duplicates detectable via the four blocking strategies used. Duplicates that share none of these signals — different phone, no email, not on a team together — are invisible to this analysis. A name-only blocking pass (same last name + similar first name + same firm) would surface additional candidates at the cost of more false positives to review.
